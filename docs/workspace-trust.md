# Kogg Workspace Trust

Kogg asks whether you trust a workspace before it enables actions that can execute code, including tasks, debugging, terminals, and extensions.

- Trust folders you created or whose contents and source you have reviewed.
- Keep downloaded, generated, or unfamiliar repositories restricted until you inspect them.
- Marketplace packages remain subject to Kogg signature and policy verification even in trusted workspaces.
- Ranex and provider credentials are never made available to workspace extensions.

Use **Manage Workspace Trust** from the command palette to review or change the current decision.
